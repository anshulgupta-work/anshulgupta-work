## 👨‍💻 About Me

My journey into analytics didn't begin with code—it began with conversations.

Starting my career in Business Development, I spent over a year understanding customer needs, solving problems, and learning what drives business decisions. While interacting with customers and stakeholders, I became increasingly curious about the **"why" behind the numbers.**

That curiosity led me to pursue an MBA in Business Analytics, where I discovered how data can transform business decisions. Today, I enjoy combining business thinking with analytics to uncover insights, solve real-world problems, and build solutions that create measurable impact.

Whether it's analyzing customer behaviour, building dashboards, developing machine learning models, or exploring business strategy, I believe the best decisions are made when data and business understanding work together.
